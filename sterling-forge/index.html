Privacy Policy · 2026-11-25

Who We Are

Sterling Forge (the “App”) is published by DYKYI MIACH TOV (“we”, “our”). Below we explain, in plain language, what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have.

§

Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, the following information may be collected:

  • Device Information (e.g., device model, operating system, device identifiers)
  • Purchase information — if you buy an item in the App, Google Play sends the App a record of that purchase (order number, the item bought, the time of purchase and a purchase token). We never receive your payment card, bank or billing details.
  • Install attribution data — when you open the App, our attribution provider AppsFlyer receives your device's advertising ID, basic device and app information (model, OS version, App version, language), your IP address (used to derive your country) and the Google Play install referrer. See the Install Attribution section.

§

Why We Use This Data

This data is used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Deliver and restore the items you buy, and prevent fraudulent or duplicate purchases
  • Measure which advertising campaigns brought new players to the App, and detect fraudulent installs

§

Content You Create in the App

Your game progress, scores, unlocked levels, in-game coin balance and settings are saved on your device so you can continue where you left off.

This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see it. It is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include it in your own Google backup, which we cannot access.

§

Advertising

Sterling Forge does not display advertising and does not share your data with advertising networks for ad targeting. We do, however, use an install attribution provider to measure which advertising campaigns bring new players to the App — see the Install Attribution section.

§

Purchases

Sterling Forge is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases of virtual coins. All payments are processed by Google Play (Google LLC): your payment details are handled by Google under the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy, and are never shared with us.

  • What we receive: only the purchase record described in the Information Collected Automatically section, which the App uses to add the coins to your balance and to finish purchases that were interrupted. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.
  • Virtual coins have no real-money value, cannot be exchanged for cash and can only be used inside the App. They are stored on your device: uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes them.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies.
  • Parents: every purchase needs the approval of the Google account holder. You can require a password or approval for purchases in the Google Play app (Settings → Authentication, or Google Family Link).

§

Install Attribution

Sterling Forge uses AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd.) to measure how players found the App — for example, which advertising campaign led to an install — and to detect fraudulent installs. AppsFlyer processes the data described in the Information Collected Automatically section on our behalf and only on our instructions. We record installs and app sessions only: no gameplay events, no purchase details and no contact information are sent. The App sends this data when it is opened. You can limit it at any time by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings, and you can ask us to delete the data held about your device (see the Contact section). AppsFlyer handles this data under the AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy.

§

Other Services We Use

We may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (for example, to measure app performance and fix bugs). These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data.

§

How Long We Keep Data

We retain collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, and it is automatically deleted within 90 days. You may also request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the details below, and we will process your request promptly. What you keep in the App yourself (see the Content You Create in the App section) stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Purchase records the App receives are kept on your device only for as long as the App is installed; Google keeps its own payment records under Google's policies (see the Purchases section).

§

Notifications and Permissions

The App asks only for what its features need:

  • Notifications — to show the optional reminders you set up. They are scheduled on your device, and you can turn them off in the App or in your Android settings.
  • Internet access — to process purchases through Google Play and measure installs with AppsFlyer. The App's own features work without an account.

§

Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
